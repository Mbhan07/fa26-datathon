Click any bed to see the patient's true vitals, what the oximeter displays, and how likely that reading is to hide their risk.

Teaching dataset and simulation, not a clinical tool. True oxygen = the dataset's SpO2 (no blood gas available). Oximeter error: OpenOximetry Repository (UCSF Hypoxia Lab, PhysioNet), healthy volunteers in controlled lab desaturation; error sampled by measured Monk skin tone and true-oxygen band. Each bed uses one fixed random draw so readings move consistently as bias increases; "chance of downgrade" is from 1,000 simulated readings. Extra overestimation is a hypothetical scenario: hospital studies report larger gaps than the lab (Sjoding et al., NEJM 2020). Risk = NEWS2 (Royal College of Physicians, 2017). Second check = gradient-boosting model trained without SpO2, out-of-fold.
